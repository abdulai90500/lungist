Welcome to LUNGICEST

A college rooted in community. Focused on the future.

Lungi College of Education Science & Technology is committed to making relevant learning and practical skills part of a brighter future for local communities.

We believe education can open doors—to further study, meaningful work and community progress. Our ambition is to help every learner discover what they can contribute.

Our purpose

Guided by a vision.
Grounded in a mission.

Our vision 01

LUNGICEST is a synergist to engage partners in bringing creative and innovative educational solutions to individuals, employers, and communities to transform challenges into opportunities.

Our mission 02

Our mission is to provide relevant mainstream, technical, vocational education and training to support students’ goals, a skilled work force and the economic dynamics of our communities.
